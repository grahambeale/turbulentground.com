I've spent twenty-five years building product and design teams, at agencies, at scale-ups, at organisations going through significant change. The consistent thing I noticed is that the teams that handle change well are never the ones who figured it out alone. They are the ones who found others going through the same thing, compared notes, and stopped feeling like the uncertainty was a personal failing.

When AI started disrupting product work, not as a concept, but as something that changed how teams operated, I noticed the same pattern. People were privately confused, publicly confident, and largely isolated in both. Nobody was having the honest conversation.

Turbulent Ground is where that conversation happens. The Care Capital framework is the research backbone, a structured way to measure what makes teams ready to work with AI rather than just faster at the wrong things.
