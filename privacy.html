Privacy notice

This notice covers the Turbulent Ground website. If you take part in the AI shift research study, the study privacy notice also applies and explains the study in full.

Who is responsible for your data

Turbulent Ground is run by me, Graham Beale. It is not a company or a separate legal entity. I am the data controller, the person legally responsible for how your information is collected and used.

ICO registration reference: ZC232927. You can check the public register at ico.org.uk/register.

For anything about your information, email graham@turbulentground.com.

What this site collects, and why

WhatWhyLegal basis
Anonymous usage statistics from Plausible Analytics: pages viewed, referring site, broad location from your IP address (not stored), device type, and site events such as button clicks or page errorsTo understand how the site is used and to spot problemsLegitimate interests
Feedback you send with the feedback button: your message, the address of the page you were on and the time you sent itTo fix problems and improve the siteLegitimate interests
Diagnostic submissions (the Care Capital diagnostic, which is no longer promoted on this site): your work email, optional name and team name, your 25 answers and scores, optional role, organisation size, sector and country, consent choices, and any tracked-link sourceTo send results you asked for and produce aggregate analysisConsent

Plausible does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Please don't include personal details in feedback. If you do, I will treat them as described here and delete anything that isn't needed.

Diagnostic data

If you submitted the diagnostic, your email domain was used to group responses by organisation. No organisation-level result is calculated until at least three submissions exist for that domain, and consumer email domains (such as Gmail or Outlook) are never grouped. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing me.

Where your data goes

  • Vercel hosts the site and processes request logs for a short period.
  • Plausible Analytics (EU-hosted) processes anonymous usage statistics.
  • Airtable stores feedback and diagnostic submissions. Airtable's servers are in the United States and transfers are covered by its data processing agreement and the UK International Data Transfer Agreement framework.
  • Resend sends any results emails you requested.

I do not sell your data or share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

How long I keep it

  • Feedback: until the issue it raises is dealt with, and no longer than two years.
  • Diagnostic submissions: three years from submission, or until you ask me to delete them, whichever comes first.
  • Plausible statistics are aggregate and contain no personal data.

Your rights, and how to use them

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to how it is used, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Email graham@turbulentground.com and I will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
