## D06 amendment — persistent link and private invitation composition

**Outcome:** ACT with changes

After successful completion, each participant receives a persistent personal
referral link with an exclusive public referral identifier. The participant may
reuse that link in public posts or private messages across channels.

The completion experience and requested results/comparison email may also help
the participant prepare a private invitation. They may optionally enter a
recipient's first name and, for email, an email address. Turbulent Ground uses
those values only in the participant's browser to prepare the message. It does
not retain them, add the recipient to a contact list or send an unsolicited
invitation itself.

Approval of revision 3 would authorise the next design and architecture review
only. It would not authorise implementation, participant contact or release.
