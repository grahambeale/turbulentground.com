# Phase 3.1 proposed participant wording

**Status:** Draft for Graham's review. This wording is not live and does not replace the current privacy notice.

## Public start

### Who is responsible for this study

I am Graham Beale, and this is a personal research project rather than work
carried out by a company. I am responsible for deciding how the personal data
collected for the study is used and protected. The full privacy notice explains
how to contact me and exercise your data rights.

### Why we ask for your name and email

Your name helps make the study feel personal. Your email lets us send your private return link, your results if you request them, and one reminder if you begin but do not finish. We will not add you to study updates unless you choose that separately.

### Incomplete response retention

If you start but do not complete the survey, I will keep your answers and contact details for 14 days so you can return. I will send one reminder after seven days. If you do not complete the survey, I will automatically delete the incomplete response and its contact details after 14 days. I do this so the research data I keep is data you have confirmed you want included in the study.

### Referral attribution

If you arrived through another participant's link, I may record that link as the first referral connected with your participation. The person who shared it will not see your name, email, answers or whether you personally completed the survey.

### Required confirmation

I am 18 or over. I have read the privacy information and agree to take part in this research study.

### Separate optional email choice

Send me occasional study emails with findings, follow-up questions and opportunities to contribute. Graham will email me no more than once every 30 days, and I can unsubscribe at any time.

## Post-completion sharing

### Your personal sharing link

Your link helps us understand how the study travels. Anyone can use it to start the survey, but it does not reveal your identity, answers or results.

### Private invitation fields

The name, email address and message you enter here stay in this browser. They are not sent to Turbulent Ground, saved with your response or included in analytics. Your chosen email or social app handles the message when you continue.
