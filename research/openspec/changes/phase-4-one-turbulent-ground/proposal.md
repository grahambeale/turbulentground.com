# Phase 4: One Turbulent Ground — proposal

## Why

Turbulent Ground currently presents one successful product through two historical structures: a wider site and a research area. Phase 4 should make the Phase 3 research-led experience the centre of the domain without losing entry paths, participant protections, historical validity or useful legacy content.

## Evidence

See `evidence-snapshot.md`. The governing source is Airtable record `FB-20260930-PHASE4`.

## Interpretation

This is a product consolidation, not a cosmetic homepage replacement and not a new questionnaire version. Public access, personal invitations and referrals are different ways into the same product. The safest move is a staged migration with compatibility routes and a single model-neutral change process.

## Desired outcome

Someone arriving anywhere on the Turbulent Ground domain understands one proposition and can enter the appropriate journey. Existing invitation, referral, resume and results links continue to work. Graham can ask either Claude Code or Codex for a product change and receive the same governed intake, specification, approval, implementation and release process.

## What changes

1. Make the Phase 3 product model the canonical domain experience and treat this consolidation as Phase 4.
2. Define `/` as the canonical public entry while preserving `/research` through a compatible route or redirect.
3. Preserve invite-only, referral, secure-return, results and email journeys as supported entry or continuation states.
4. Keep `/learnings` as the editorial/library area.
5. Stop promoting Phase 2/Care Capital as a parallel product; retain legacy URLs until inventory and redirect acceptance tests pass.
6. Introduce one canonical, model-neutral operating layer for product work and make Airtable plus OpenSpec mandatory gates.
7. Separate product consolidation from questionnaire versioning so historical response comparability remains intact.

## Capabilities

- Canonical public entry and routing
- Invite-only participation
- Referral recognition and attribution
- Save-and-return and reminder email continuity
- Consent, privacy, retention and withdrawal continuity
- Completion, results and benchmark continuity
- Feedback and operational administration
- Legacy-route compatibility and rollback
- Unified Claude/Codex product governance

## Impact

The eventual implementation can affect root and research routes, site navigation, metadata and social previews, analytics, participant emails, API endpoints, documentation, agent instructions, deployment verification and legacy redirects. Each implementation work item must be independently reviewable and separately release-approved.
