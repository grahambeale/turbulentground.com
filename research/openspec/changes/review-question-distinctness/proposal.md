# Review the distinctness of the approved questions — proposal revision 1

## Evidence
One participant found similar questions acceptable and the survey good. Related repetition records are identified in the existing assessment, but are not merged or counted as independently verified new evidence in this draft.

## Interpretation
Rationale v1.1 already documents the purpose of each pair and H29 flags possible Autonomy overlap. Similar answers are not proof of duplication or evidence that a person is answering carelessly. The approved revision supersedes the earlier generic audit baseline.

## Desired outcome
Determine which approved pairs people can distinguish and which need further investigation while preserving useful coverage.

## Scope and impact
Map all 12 approved pairs to their documented meanings. Use paraphrase and contrast prompts, prioritising Autonomy, Time and workload, and reciprocal Trust/Being seen. Record alternative explanations for similarity. Keep historical and revised instruments separate.

Drafting authorises no design or prototype. Scoring, results and production release remain outside this proposal.
