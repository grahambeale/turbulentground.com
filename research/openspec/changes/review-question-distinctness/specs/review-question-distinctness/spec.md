# Review the distinctness of the approved questions — specification revision 1

## Evidence
One participant found similar questions acceptable and the survey good. Related repetition records are identified in the existing assessment, but are not merged or counted as independently verified new evidence in this draft.

## Interpretation
Rationale v1.1 already documents the purpose of each pair and H29 flags possible Autonomy overlap. Similar answers are not proof of duplication or evidence that a person is answering carelessly. The approved revision supersedes the earlier generic audit baseline.

## Desired outcome
Determine which approved pairs people can distinguish and which need further investigation while preserving useful coverage.

## Requirements
Map all 12 approved pairs to their documented meanings. Use paraphrase and contrast prompts, prioritising Autonomy, Time and workload, and reciprocal Trust/Being seen. Record alternative explanations for similarity. Keep historical and revised instruments separate.

## Exact wording or review prompts
Review prompts: What is each statement asking about? How is the second different from the first? Can you imagine agreeing with one and disagreeing with the other? Is either difficult to apply to your work?

## Constraints
Preserve approved v1.1 wording, historical item meanings, instrument versions, consent and privacy commitments. No participant data in repository artifacts. Use existing styling for later purpose-copy previews and maintain WCAG 2.2 AA. Drafting creates no participant-facing implementation.

## Assumptions
A small comprehension review can expose ambiguity but cannot statistically validate the instrument.

## Unknowns
Which pairs remain confusing in the revised wording? Is there a sufficient compatible sample for descriptive correlations or abandonment review? This run has not inspected participant response data.

## Non-goals
Autonomous approval, live question additions or rewrites, new scoring or benchmark rules, releasing the paired preview, labelling respondents careless, and claiming population effects from individual comments.

## Acceptance criteria
Graham approves the review scope. Produce a pair-by-pair worksheet with meaning, observed paraphrases, overlap and recommendation. Do not flag individuals as careless from repeated scores. Quantitative analysis waits for an explicitly defined adequate same-version sample and privacy-safe plan. Removals or rewrites require a new approved revision.
